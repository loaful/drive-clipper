Moving or renaming your folder in Google Drive

OKF Clipper tracks your save folder by its Google Drive ID, so you can freely move or rename WebClips anywhere in Google Drive without breaking future clips. Use Create / Switch Folder only if you want to start saving into a brand-new folder.

Tags & Frontmatter

Set default tags for new clips and choose which metadata fields appear in YAML frontmatter.

Press Enter or comma to add a tag. Click × or press Backspace to remove. Used as initial defaults — the Popup remembers your last-used tags until you change or clear them with ×.
